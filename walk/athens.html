Athens in one walk 8 sights, 55 minutes

A self-guided walking tour of the Athens Old Town: 8 sights on one 3.7 km loop, about 55 minutes on foot, back where you started.

No two days to spare? Here is the short version: the big sights in one loop. I would take longer. I always take longer.

The stops

8 sights in Athens, in walking order.

Each stop says how far the next one is and which streets take you there.

  1. 1. Plateia Syntagmatos

    the start

    Plateia Syntagmatos on your map. At the top end of the square two men stand very still, dressed like nobody else in Athens, and now and then they move. It is worth waiting for. The square's name is a single word a crowd once demanded. I also stand very still. Nobody gathers to look at me.

    Next: Mitropolitikos Naos Athinon, 480 m, about 7 min, along Plateia Syntagmatos, Ermou, Petraki, Mitropoleos.

  2. 2. Mitropolitikos Naos Athinon

    about 7 minutes of walking from the start

    Plateia Mitropoleos on your map: a large church on a large square, and right beside it a very small one. The city has a saying about the pair, and it is not really about buildings. The large one has a past of its own: what went into its walls, and where it was supposed to stand. I eat the square between them and leave both.

    Next: Panagia Kapnikarea, 210 m, about 3 min, along Evangelistrias, Ermou.

  3. 3. Panagia Kapnikarea

    about 10 minutes of walking from the start

    A small church sitting in the middle of a street: Kapnikarea on your map. Why it is still there is a story with a royal family in it. Its name is a riddle with money in it, and the art inside is far younger than the walls. Three answers, and I am keeping all of them.

    Next: Tzami Tzistaraki, 280 m, about 4 min, along Ermou, Kirykeiou, Pandrosou.

  4. 4. Tzami Tzistaraki

    about 15 minutes of walking from the start

    Tzami Tzistaraki on your map, standing on Monastiraki Square under a dome, with the square's crowd all round it. The man who put it up made one choice of material that cost him dearly. I am not saying which. I take building material seriously too.

    Next: Poikili Stoa, 350 m, about 5 min, along Ifaistou, Agiou Filippou.

  5. 5. Poikili Stoa

    about 20 minutes of walking from the start

    Poikili Stoa on your map. From Adrianou you look over a fence into a dig: foundations, a few stone blocks, nothing that shouts. A word you have almost certainly used started in these few stones. Which one, I am not telling. Stones are good at keeping things to themselves. I would know.

    Next: Plateia Koumoundourou, 710 m, about 11 min, along Adrianou, Thiseiou, Ermou, Ivis, Lepeniotou, Ogygou, Kriezi, Plateia Eleftherias, Plateia Koumoundourou.

  6. Lunch break

    Usurum, Lepeniotou 19, about 10 m off the route, open Mon–Fri 07:30–18:00, Sat–Sun 07:30–19:00.

    On Lepeniotou, five minutes from Monastiraki by the house's own count. More on what to eat in Athens.

  7. 6. Plateia Koumoundourou

    about 30 minutes of walking from the start

    Koumoundourou Square looks like an ordinary city square. Ordinary squares are my favourite: a whole plate of stone at once. This one has had more lives than it lets on, and a newspaper once poked fun at what people were calling it. That is all I am saying.

    Next: Varvakeios Agora, 580 m, about 9 min, along Plateia Koumoundourou, Plateia Eleftherias, Evripidou, Aristogeitonos.

  8. 7. Varvakeios Agora

    about 39 minutes of walking from the start · inside open Mon–Sat 07:00–18:00, Sun closed

    The city's central market, Varvakeios Agora on your map. Noise everywhere, and a name for the place that the city borrowed from another one. The market's own name goes back to a man who got rich in a way you would not guess, a long sea journey from here. I eat only the street outside. The rest is yours to look at.

    Next: Ethniko Istoriko Mouseio, 710 m, about 11 min, along Aristogeitonos, Evripidou, Praxitelous, Kolokotroni.

  9. 8. Ethniko Istoriko Mouseio

    about 50 minutes of walking from the start · inside open Tue–Sun 08:30–14:30, Mon closed

    Ethniko Istoriko Mouseio on your map: a grand old house on Stadiou with a horseman on a pedestal in front of it. Look at which way he is turned, then at his arm: the city made a joke out of the pair, and I am keeping it for when you get there. The joke stands outside, so it needs no ticket. Neither do I.

    Back to the start: Plateia Syntagmatos, 340 m, about 5 min, along Kolokotroni.

Every stop counts from the street; the hours are for going inside. Opening hours from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors. Check before you go.
