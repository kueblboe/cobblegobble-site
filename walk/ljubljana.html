Ljubljana in one walk 8 sights, 60 minutes

A self-guided walking tour of the Ljubljana Old Town: 8 sights on one 4.0 km loop, about 60 minutes on foot, back where you started.

No two days to spare? Here is the short version: the big sights in one loop. I would take longer. I always take longer.

The stops

8 sights in Ljubljana, in walking order.

Each stop says how far the next one is and which streets take you there.

  1. 1. Ljubljanski grad

    the start

    The one uphill stretch in this level, and there is no way round it. A lane up there is named Ulica na grad, and the name is the whole promise. Whatever is waiting on the terrace, you will have earned it a step at a time. This is the one I take in small bites.

    Next: Cerkev sv. Jakoba, 660 m, about 10 min, along Grajska planota, Mačja steza, Reber, Stari trg, Levstikov trg.

  2. 2. Cerkev sv. Jakoba

    about 10 minutes of walking from the start

    The far south end of the walk, and the reason to go is the lane that takes you there. From Stari trg the roofline steps down and the way gets tighter, and Gornji trg is the oldest slice of the Old Town left. Then the belfry is opposite you, across the road.

    Next: Robbov vodnjak, 490 m, about 7 min, along Levstikov trg, Stari trg, Mestni trg.

  3. 3. Robbov vodnjak

    about 17 minutes of walking from the start

    It sits in front of the town hall and everybody points a camera at the upper end of it. Look down instead. The outline of the basin repeats the old seal of the town, so the plan at your feet is older than anything above it.

    Next: Narodna in univerzitetna knjižnica, 430 m, about 7 min, along Mestni trg, Novi trg.

  4. 4. Narodna in univerzitetna knjižnica

    about 24 minutes of walking from the start · open Mon–Fri 08:00–20:00, Sat 09:00–14:00, Sun closed

    From outside this is the plainest thing on the walk: a brick box with rough blocks shoved out of the wall at random, except none of it is random. It sits in the far south-west corner of the level, which is the quietest end of it. Go in the front way and lift your head before you do anything else.

    Next: Prešernov trg, 420 m, about 6 min, along Gosposka ulica, Kongresni trg, Wolfova ulica.

  5. 5. Prešernov trg

    about 30 minutes of walking from the start

    Everything in this level hinges here. Three bridges leave the square shoulder to shoulder as one crossing, the Tromostovje, and every route through the Old Town wants to use them. Stop in the middle and count the ways out.

    Next: Konjički, 270 m, about 4 min, along Wolfova ulica, Miklošičeva cesta.

  6. 6. Konjički

    about 34 minutes of walking from the start

    The northernmost pin, and the easiest one to walk straight past. Whatever you are looking for is at knee height, not at eye height, and that is the whole trick of it. Knee height I know about. It is where I live.

    Next: Žitni most, 690 m, about 10 min, along Prečna ulica, Trubarjeva cesta, Petkovškovo nabrežje.

  7. Lunch break

    Gostilna Šestica, Slovenska cesta 40, about 180 m off the route, open Mon–Fri 10:00–23:00, Sat 12:00–23:00, Sun 12:00–17:00.

    The oldest house in town still feeding people, in business since 1776, and named after nothing grander than a street number. More on what to eat in Ljubljana.

  8. 7. Žitni most

    about 44 minutes of walking from the start

    The east end, the last pin before this level runs out. Cars never got a say here. Go out onto it and turn back toward the ridge: the covered market arcade runs along the bank in one unbroken line, which you cannot see from the bank itself.

    Next: Vodnikov trg, 400 m, about 6 min, along Žitni most, Poljanski nasip, Vodnikov trg.

  9. 8. Vodnikov trg

    about 50 minutes of walking from the start

    A market fills this square most mornings, so you smell it before you see anything. Come early. The stalls reach right in under the arcade by the river, and from here the whole level runs downhill in every direction I care about.

    Back to the start: Ljubljanski grad, 600 m, about 9 min, along Vodnikov trg, Ovinki, Grajska planota.
