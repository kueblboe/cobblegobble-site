Girona in one walk 8 sights, 35 minutes

A self-guided walking tour of the Girona Old Town: 8 sights on one 2.4 km loop, about 35 minutes on foot, back where you started.

No two days to spare? Here is the short version: the big sights in one loop. I would take longer. I always take longer.

The stops

8 sights in Girona, in walking order.

Each stop says how far the next one is and which streets take you there.

  1. 1. Catedral de Girona

    the start

    The far corner of the walk, and the one I put last on the plate. The square in front is where to stand, not the doorway. From the far side of it the whole west front is in one piece. From the doorway it never is.

    Next: Basilica de Sant Feliu, 200 m, about 3 min, along Carrer dels Manaies, Pujada del Rei Martí.

  2. 2. Basilica de Sant Feliu

    about 3 minutes of walking from the start

    The other big church, down at the north end where the ground finally lets go. Go once round the outside before you go in. Whoever assembled this took what was lying about, and the outside is where that shows.

    Next: Església de Sant Pere de Galligants, 210 m, about 3 min, along Pujada del Rei Martí.

  3. 3. Església de Sant Pere de Galligants

    about 6 minutes of walking from the start

    The far north tip of the level, out past where the Old Town stopped, on the other side of the stream it is named after. It is the only thing on this route that had room to spread sideways, and it used all of it. Look at the front from across the little square.

    Next: Banys Àrabs, 100 m, about 2 min.

  4. 4. Banys Àrabs

    about 8 minutes of walking from the start · open Mon–Sat 10:00–18:00, Sun 10:00–14:00

    Small, easy to miss, off a lane you will already be walking. Do not stop in the first chamber. The thing everybody remembers is one further in, and I am not going to tell you what it is.

    Next: Torre de Sant Domenec, 510 m, about 8 min, along Passeig de la Reina Joana, Passeig de la Muralla.

  5. 5. Torre de Sant Domenec

    about 15 minutes of walking from the start

    The east edge of the level, and the only stretch of it where you go along the top of the defences instead of underneath them. Get up at one end and stay up: the ways back down are further apart than you would like. Town on one side, open country on the other.

    Next: Pont de Pedra, 680 m, about 10 min, along Passeig de la Muralla, Carreró de la Torre dels Socors, Carrer Nou del Teatre, Pujada del Pont de Pedra, Pont de Pedra.

  6. Lunch break

    La Fábrica, Carrer de la Llebre 3, about 100 m off the route, open Mon–Sun 09:00–16:00.

    An old carpenter's workshop on the Carrer de la Llebre, now a cool café with stone walls, opened by a retired pro cyclist and his wife. More on what to eat in Girona.

  7. 6. Pont de Pedra

    about 25 minutes of walking from the start

    The wide one, the one the whole city crosses without looking. Stop halfway and turn upriver. That row of houses standing in the water, painted every colour anybody had to hand, is the picture that ends up on the postcards, and this is the spot it is taken from.

    Next: Pont de les Peixateries Velles, 300 m, about 4 min, along Pont de Pedra, Pont de les Peixateries Velles.

  8. 7. Pont de les Peixateries Velles

    about 30 minutes of walking from the start

    One of the crossings, and by a long way the busiest. Go early or go late; around midday you will be queueing for the chance to stand still on it. It is iron, it rings under your feet, and it is the only thing on this level that answers back.

    Next: Museu d'Història dels Jueus, 250 m, about 4 min, along Pont de les Peixateries Velles, Carrer de l'Argenteria.

  9. 8. Museu d'Història dels Jueus

    about 34 minutes of walking from the start

    Its door is on the Carrer de la Força, and you will be on the Carrer de la Força whether you plan to be or not. Everything round about is the reason to come. This is the room that explains it. Do the outside first and the inside second: that order matters.

    Back to the start: Catedral de Girona, 210 m, about 3 min, along Carrer de la Força, Pujada de la Catedral.
