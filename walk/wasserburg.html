A self-guided walking tour of the Wasserburg am Inn Old Town: 8 sights on one 2.1 km loop, about 30 minutes on foot, back where you started.

No two days to spare? Here is the short version: the big sights in one loop. I would take longer. I always take longer.

The stops

8 sights in Wasserburg am Inn, in walking order.

Each stop says how far the next one is and which streets take you there.

  1. 1. Wasserburg

    the start

    Stepped gables on the neck of land, right where the peninsula stops being a peninsula. Whoever held this held the bridge, the salt and the town. The lane called Auf der Burg runs straight through it.

    Next: St. Jakob, 170 m, about 3 min, along Auf der Burg, Kirchhofplatz.

  2. 2. St. Jakob

    about 3 minutes of walking from the start

    Unrendered brick with a tower of tuff blocks, set in the crook between castle hill and town, out of the water's reach. The parish church stands apart from the market on purpose.

    Next: Mauthaus, 90 m, about 1 min, along Schustergasse.

  3. 3. Mauthaus

    about 4 minutes of walking from the start

    A corner house at the market with vaulted arcades at street level, standing where the road bends. Money changed hands here for centuries.

    Next: Brucktor, 60 m, about 1 min, along Bruckgasse.

  4. 4. Brucktor

    about 5 minutes of walking from the start

    The gate you pass through on the way to the bridge, four storeys of it, wedged between the old hospital and the town house. Look up as you go under.

    Next: Rathaus, 150 m, about 2 min, along Bruckgasse.

  5. 5. Rathaus

    about 7 minutes of walking from the start

    Two stepped gables of unequal height on Marienplatz, one wider and taller than its neighbour. Step inside and look down. I am not telling you why.

    Next: Frauenkirche, 0 m, about 1 min.

  6. 6. Frauenkirche

    about 7 minutes of walking from the start

    The smaller church, standing right in the market space instead of beside it, with a slim tower over a hall you walk through.

    Next: Hungerturm, 350 m, about 5 min, along Salzsenderzeile.

  7. Lunch break

    Boulevard 10, Ledererzeile 10, about 60 m off the route, open Wed–Sun 09:00–18:00, Mon–Tue closed.

    Same family, same house on the Ledererzeile since the 1950s. More on what to eat in Wasserburg am Inn.

  8. 7. Hungerturm

    about 12 minutes of walking from the start

    A square tower with battlements at the top of Im Hag, planted where the town's defences turned a corner. It has windows now, which tells you most of what became of it.

    Next: Pulverturm, 650 m, about 10 min, along Im Hag, Schopperstattweg, Holzhofweg.

  9. 8. Pulverturm

    about 22 minutes of walking from the start

    A squat tower at the western corner of the old defences. The street that runs past it gives the game away, which saves me the trouble.

    Back to the start: Wasserburg, 660 m, about 10 min, along Schopperstattweg, Unter der Schanz, Münchner Straße, Auf der Burg.
