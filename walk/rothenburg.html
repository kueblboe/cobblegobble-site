Rothenburg ob der Tauber in one walk 8 sights, 45 minutes

A self-guided walking tour of the Rothenburg ob der Tauber Old Town: 8 sights on one 3.1 km loop, about 45 minutes on foot, back where you started.

No two days to spare? Here is the short version: the big sights in one loop. I would take longer. I always take longer.

The stops

8 sights in Rothenburg ob der Tauber, in walking order.

Each stop says how far the next one is and which streets take you there.

  1. 1. St. Jakob

    the start

    The town church. I tried to take a bite and it was too tall. Go in and keep going until you have to climb. I am not telling you what waits there.

    Next: Franziskanerkirche, 210 m, about 3 min, along Kirchgasse, Herrngasse.

  2. 2. Franziskanerkirche

    about 3 minutes of walking from the start

    After 1805 this church housed the salt office, and only from 1871 was it a Protestant church. Salt first, prayers later: a building after my own heart. Walk round it first, then step in. I am not telling you what to look for.

    Next: Rathausturm, 160 m, about 2 min, along Herrngasse.

  3. 3. Rathausturm

    about 6 minutes of walking from the start · open Mon–Sun 09:30–12:30, 13:00–17:00

    The tall one over the market square. From the top the whole town lies spread out like a buffet. I counted the roofs instead of eating them. Almost. I am not telling you how many stairs.

    Next: Mittelalterliches Kriminalmuseum, 190 m, about 3 min, along Marktplatz, Obere Schmiedgasse.

  4. 4. Mittelalterliches Kriminalmuseum

    about 8 minutes of walking from the start

    A big old house on the Burggasse, full of other people's rules. Rules are not edible. I checked. I am not telling you what else is inside.

    Next: Brunnen am Plönlein, 200 m, about 3 min, along Burggasse, Untere Schmiedgasse.

  5. 5. Brunnen am Plönlein

    about 11 minutes of walking from the start

    A small corner the town is rather proud of. I came for the cobbles and stayed for the view. Stand still for once and look down both lanes. I am not telling you what you will recognise.

    Next: Stöberleinsturm, 450 m, about 7 min, along Kobolzeller Steige, Mühlacker.

  6. Lunch break

    Ivy’s Deli, Spitalgasse 6, about 30 m off the route, open Mon 08:30–16:00, Thu–Sat 08:30–16:00, Sun 09:00–16:00, Tue–Wed closed.

    A small deli on the Spitalgasse, a few steps south of the Plönlein. More on what to eat in Rothenburg ob der Tauber.

  7. 6. Stöberleinsturm

    about 18 minutes of walking from the start

    A tower in the wall wearing a hat far too pointy for its size. It also goes by a second name, and I am not telling you that one either.

    Next: Rödertor, 810 m, about 12 min, along Spitalhof, Sterngasse, Röderschütt.

  8. 7. Rödertor

    about 30 minutes of walking from the start

    One of the old gates in the wall, and one you can still climb. Look at it twice: once from inside the town, once from outside. I am not telling you why.

    Next: St.-Wolfgangs-Kirche, 770 m, about 11 min, along Rödergasse, Pfeifersgäßchen, Pfarrgasse, Judengasse, Klingengasse.

  9. 8. St.-Wolfgangs-Kirche

    about 42 minutes of walking from the start

    A church set into the town wall. It looks more like it is keeping watch than praying. Walk round it slowly. I am not telling you what is underneath.

    Back to the start: St. Jakob, 290 m, about 4 min, along Klingengasse.
