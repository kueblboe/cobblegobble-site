Budapest in one walk 7 sights, 90 minutes

A self-guided walking tour of the Budapest Old Town: 7 sights on one 5.9 km loop, about 90 minutes on foot, back where you started.

No two days to spare? Here is the short version: the big sights in one loop. I would take longer. I always take longer.

The stops

7 sights in Budapest, in walking order.

Each stop says how far the next one is and which streets take you there.

  1. 1. Budavári Palota

    the start

    Come in through the Oroszlános udvar. Four lions on the gate, and not one of them moves aside. Then out onto the Savoyai terasz and look down: from there you can see everything you have eaten today.

    Next: Citadella, 1,500 m, about 22 min, along Szent György utca, Savoyai terasz, Ybl Miklós tér, Döbrentei utca.

  2. 2. Citadella

    about 22 minutes of walking from the start

    The highest thing on this level. Do not go straight up, take the Citadella sétány round the back: longer, shadier, and the view arrives all at once at the end instead of in bites. Save this one for last.

    Next: Magyarok Nagyasszonya Sziklatemplom, 700 m, about 11 min, along Citadella sétány, Citadella lejtő.

  3. 3. Magyarok Nagyasszonya Sziklatemplom

    about 33 minutes of walking from the start

    This one is not on the hillside, it is in it. At the Buda end of Szabadság híd, look for the opening in the crag; there is barely a front to find. I like a building that is mostly not there.

    Next: Központi Vásárcsarnok, 600 m, about 9 min.

  4. 4. Központi Vásárcsarnok

    about 42 minutes of walking from the start · open Mon–Fri 06:00–18:00, Sat 06:00–16:00, Sun 10:00–16:00

    The one thing on this level that agrees with me about what a city is for. Fish downstairs, paprika and sausage and pickles by the crate on the main floor, lunch above. It sits at the Pest end of Szabadság híd and you smell it before you see it.

    Next: Erzsébet híd, 910 m, about 14 min, along Központi Vásárcsarnok, Váci utca, Erzsébet híd.

  5. 5. Erzsébet híd

    about 56 minutes of walking from the start

    The white one, and the fastest way off the Pest lanes to the foot of the hill. Stop in the middle and look upstream: both banks line up from there, and the rest of your day is laid out in front of you.

    Next: Pesti Vigadó, 760 m, about 11 min, along Erzsébet híd, Petőfi tér, Apáczai Csere János utca, Vigadó tér.

  6. 6. Pesti Vigadó

    about 67 minutes of walking from the start · open Mon–Sun 10:00–19:00

    Stand on the Dunakorzó and look up. Every hand's width of that front is decorated and no piece of it agrees with the next. Then turn round: nowhere on this bank gives you a better look at the hill you still have to get up.

    Next: Széchenyi lánchíd, 690 m, about 10 min, along Apáczai Csere János utca, Eötvös tér, Széchenyi István tér, Széchenyi lánchíd.

  7. 7. Széchenyi lánchíd

    about 77 minutes of walking from the start · open Mon–Sun 00:00–24:00

    Walk this one instead of photographing it. A lion sits at each end, and between them the deck hums under your soles in a way no picture carries. The flattest thing I get all day, and I take my time over it.

    Back to the start: Budavári Palota, 730 m, about 11 min, along Széchenyi lánchíd, Clark Ádám tér, Szent György utca.
