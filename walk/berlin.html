Berlin in one walk 8 sights, 70 minutes

A self-guided walking tour of the Berlin Old Town: 8 sights on one 4.7 km loop, about 70 minutes on foot, back where you started.

No two days to spare? Here is the short version: the big sights in one loop. I would take longer. I always take longer.

The stops

8 sights in Berlin, in walking order.

Each stop says how far the next one is and which streets take you there.

  1. 1. Brandenburger Tor

    the start · open Mon–Sun 00:00–24:00

    The west end of the walk, and the only landmark here with a chariot parked on the roof. Everyone stops. I keep chewing.

    Next: Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin, 950 m, about 14 min, along Unter den Linden, Friedrichstraße.

  2. 2. Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin

    about 14 minutes of walking from the start · open Mon–Sat 08:00–22:00, Sun 10:00–18:00

    It takes up so much of the avenue that you walk its front for a while before you reach a door. Good dots. Long building.

    Next: Pergamonmuseum, 800 m, about 12 min, along Charlottenstraße, Bodestraße.

  3. 3. Pergamonmuseum

    about 26 minutes of walking from the start

    One of five museums packed onto a single island, with a channel of water down one side. The island is the densest stretch of the walk. Bring an appetite.

    Next: Berliner Dom, 320 m, about 5 min, along Bodestraße, Am Lustgarten.

  4. 4. Berliner Dom

    about 31 minutes of walking from the start

    It sits on the island with a lawn in front of it, and the roof has gone green. Copper does that, slowly. I approve of slowly.

    Next: Neue Wache, 390 m, about 6 min, along Am Lustgarten.

  5. 5. Neue Wache

    about 37 minutes of walking from the start

    Small, square, and set well back among the trees, where the avenue is at its widest. Easy to walk past. I never do.

    Next: Hausvogteiplatz, 490 m, about 7 min, along Oberwallstraße.

  6. 6. Hausvogteiplatz

    about 44 minutes of walking from the start

    Small, and most people cross it without ever looking down. Something at the station entrance is worth stopping for. I am not telling you what.

    Next: Gendarmenmarkt, 390 m, about 6 min, along Hausvogteiplatz, Taubenstraße, Gendarmenmarkt.

  7. 7. Gendarmenmarkt

    about 50 minutes of walking from the start

    A square with a building on three of its sides and nothing in the middle but space. Berlin does not usually hand you space. Take it.

    Next: Akademie der Künste, 1,180 m, about 18 min, along Gendarmenmarkt, Behrenstraße.

  8. 8. Akademie der Künste

    about 68 minutes of walking from the start · open Mon–Sun 10:00–20:00

    The one building on Pariser Platz you can see straight into. Everything else up there is stone with its face done up. This one shows you the inside.

    Back to the start: Brandenburger Tor, 180 m, about 3 min.
