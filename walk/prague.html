Prague in one walk 8 sights, 55 minutes

A self-guided walking tour of the Prague Old Town: 8 sights on one 3.7 km loop, about 55 minutes on foot, back where you started.

No two days to spare? Here is the short version: the big sights in one loop. I would take longer. I always take longer.

The stops

8 sights in Prague, in walking order.

Each stop says how far the next one is and which streets take you there.

  1. 1. Staroměstský orloj

    the start · open Mon 11:00–22:00, Tue–Sun 09:00–22:00

    There is a crowd on this square with its collective head tilted back, all facing the same wall, all waiting. They know when. Stand with them and find out, or walk on and hear it happen behind you.

    Next: Prašná brána, 490 m, about 7 min, along Staroměstské náměstí, Celetná.

  2. 2. Prašná brána

    about 7 minutes of walking from the start

    A dark tower standing on its own in the middle of the street, attached to nothing, leading nowhere. It was a way in once. The wall it belonged to is gone and it stayed behind. Its name is a plain answer to what it once contained, and I am not translating it for you.

    Next: Anežský klášter, 680 m, about 10 min, along Rybná, Haštalské náměstí, Anežská.

  3. Lunch break

    Den Noc, Templová 648/7, about 50 m off the route, open Mon–Sun 08:30–15:00.

    Lívance, the Czech pancake, made fresh every day from a family recipe. More on what to eat in Prague.

  4. 3. Anežský klášter

    about 18 minutes of walking from the start

    Right at the quiet river end of the Old Town, where the tour groups have long since turned back. A long building around a cloister, a gallery now, full of art from the Middle Ages. Walk the cloister first. Whoever put this here is the better story, and I am saving it for when you get there.

    Next: Španělská synagoga, 330 m, about 5 min, along U Obecního dvora.

  5. 4. Španělská synagoga

    about 23 minutes of walking from the start

    From the street it gives you almost nothing. The name is a puzzle and the outside will not solve it. Go in. Then come back out and look at the plain wall again.

    Next: náměstí Jana Palacha, 490 m, about 7 min, along Dušní.

  6. 5. náměstí Jana Palacha

    about 30 minutes of walking from the start

    A wide open square where the emptiness is the point, bordered on two sides by things that take themselves very seriously. It carries a person's name. That name is the heaviest thing here. Look it up somewhere that is not me.

    Next: Staroměstská mostecká věž, 380 m, about 6 min.

  7. 6. Staroměstská mostecká věž

    about 36 minutes of walking from the start

    You will walk under this one without looking at it, because the bridge is on the other side and the bridge is what you came for. Stop in the archway. Look up at the underside. Then come out and look at what is cut into the face of it.

    Next: Karlův most, 120 m, about 2 min, along Karlův most.

  8. 7. Karlův most

    about 37 minutes of walking from the start

    Everybody wants to be on this bridge at the same moment you do. Go at seven in the morning and it is almost empty. Go at noon and you will be eating elbows.

    Next: Krannerova kašna, 490 m, about 7 min, along Karlův most.

  9. 8. Krannerova kašna

    about 45 minutes of walking from the start

    Everyone walks past this one on the way to the river. It is a tall carved spire covered in small statues, and not a soul stops. Stop. Count them.

    Back to the start: Staroměstský orloj, 770 m, about 11 min, along Betlémské náměstí, Husova, Karlova.
