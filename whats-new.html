Version 1.1

Bigger dots. I kept walking past them. A missed dot is a tragedy, and in bright sun the old ones were nearly invisible. They are rounder now, ringed, and they hold their size when you zoom out.

Two ways to find what is left. Near the end of a city the last streets hide. A button frames them, and a new map mode dims everything I have already eaten.

One question instead of two. I was asking permission twice. Twice is greedy, and I am only greedy about streets.

Some bugs, eaten. A finished city stays finished when its map changes, dots stay on the street you walked them on, and Venice grew two more stories. Not delicious.
