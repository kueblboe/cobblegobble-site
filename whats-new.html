Version 1.1

The shortest way to eat everything. A button draws the walk that collects every dot a city still owes you, and I speak each turn as you reach it.

Actual food. Passau and Prague now say what to eat and where, on the map, with opening times. I cannot eat any of it.

Almost every city grew. Lanes that used to stop at nothing are joined to the streets beside them. More to chew, less doubling back.

Your harvest. One score across every city you have walked, every landmark you have picked.

Two new cities. Vilnius, and Wasserburg am Inn on a peninsula in the Inn.

Bigger dots, and some bugs. Rounder, ringed. The bugs were not delicious.
