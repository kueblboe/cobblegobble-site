How it is measured

Old Town
The historic centre as the city defines it where it publishes a boundary (an Altstadt district, a walled ring), otherwise the core a visitor walks. Every boundary is drawn on its city page. A level is sized to be walked over a trip, so in the biggest cities it covers the core, not every street of a large historic district: the ranking compares walks, not city areas.
Streets
Every street, lane, square and footbridge inside that line, from OpenStreetMap.
km on foot
One route that walks every street at least once, doubling back where a dead end makes you. Longer than the streets laid end to end.
Hours
That route at 3.5 km/h, a stroll with stops for looking, rounded to the half hour.
Streets with steps
Streets that include a stretch mapped as steps. A dash means the level was built before steps were counted, not that there are none.
Using the numbers
